## Free Trial → How the System Works

**Tabs:** Field Officers | Finance Officers | Administrators

- Step 1: Capture Expenses – Field officers record transactions and attach receipts
- Step 2: Submit for Review – Transactions are routed to finance officers for approval
- Step 3: Monitor Budgets – Managers track spending and budget utilization through dashboards

## FAQ

1. What is Tora? – A demo application showcasing real-time field expense tracking and project finance management.
2. Who is it for? – NGOs, development organizations, and teams managing finances across field locations.
3. What financial data can be tracked? – Field expenses, project budgets, approval workflows, and reporting metrics.
4. Can multiple projects be monitored? – Yes, the system supports multiple projects and teams.
5. How secure is the system? – Role-based access control and server-side validation protect all financial data.
6. Can I use Next.js for the backend? – Yes! Tora uses Next.js API routes for all backend logic.
7. What roles exist? – Field Officer, Finance Officer, and Administrator.
8. Is there an audit trail? – Yes, every financial action is logged with timestamps.
9. Can I export reports? – Yes, CSV export is available on the reports page.
10. How do I get started? – Explore the live demo or clone the GitHub repo.
